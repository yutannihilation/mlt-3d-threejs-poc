MLT columns straight to the GPU

3D flight trails around Tokyo Haneda, tiled as MapLibre Tiles (MLT) and decoded in the browser with decodeTileColumns of @maplibre/mlt-wasm. Each tile's decoded Int32Array of vertices goes to the GPU as is, in two renderers on the same MapLibre map:

  • MapLibre + Three.js: a custom layer with our own shaders; ribbons tessellated on the CPU.
  • MapLibre + deck.gl: TileLayer and stock PathLayers; ribbons extruded by PathLayer.

Flight data: adsb.lol (globe_history_2026), made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Basemap: OpenFreeMap © OpenMapTiles, data from OpenStreetMap contributors.
